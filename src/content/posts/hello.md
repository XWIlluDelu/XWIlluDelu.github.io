Welcome to Huwari, a minimal Astro blog template.

## Getting started

Edit `src/content/posts/` to add posts. Frontmatter is strictly validated:
only `title`, `published`, `updated`, `description`, `image`, `tags`,
`category`, `draft`, and `lang` are allowed.

This is a note admonition with a **custom** title.
